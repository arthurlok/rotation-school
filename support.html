Rotation School Support

Help, feedback and ideas for the Rotation School iPhone and iPad app.

Suggest a feature or report a bug

Ideas and bug reports live on the Rotation School feedback board. Upvote the ones you want most, since the most-wanted ideas get built first. Before posting, check whether someone has already suggested it and add your vote there.

Posting and voting need a free GitHub account. If you'd rather not make one, email instead.

Get help

Email rotationschool@gmail.com. Include your device, your iOS version and the app version (Settings, the gear at the top right, shows it at the bottom).

Common questions

How do I replay the app tour?

Learn → Concepts, then “Take the app tour” at the bottom of the list.

Can I stop the players from animating?

Yes. On Learn, tap the play button next to the rotation dots, or turn off “Animate rotations” in Settings. The court then jumps straight to each formation.

I changed a common system and want the original back

Open it on the Edit tab and tap Reset. Your changes to the 5-1, 4-2 and 6-2 never replace the originals.

My systems don't appear on my other device

  • Turn on iCloud sync in Settings on both devices.
  • Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account, with an internet connection.
  • Pull down on the Edit tab's list to refresh, or close and reopen the app.

How do I share a system with my team?

Turn on iCloud sync, then on the Edit tab tap ⋯ on one of your systems → “Invite people…”. Choose whether each person can only view the system or can also make changes. They open the invitation on an iPhone or iPad with Rotation School installed and iCloud sync turned on.

I opened an invitation but the system isn't there

Make sure Rotation School is installed and iCloud sync is on (the app offers to turn it on), then open the invitation link again. The system appears under “Shared with me” on the Edit tab.

Why can't I edit a system someone shared with me?

The owner invited you to view it. Ask them to let you make changes, or tap “Copy to my systems” to get your own copy that you can change freely. Your copy doesn't change theirs.

It says “Someone saved first”

Another person saved the shared system after you started editing. “Reload theirs” shows their version and drops your changes. “Overwrite” replaces their version with yours. Your changes stay safe on your device until you choose.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device. To remove your systems from iCloud, go to Settings → Delete my cloud data. To stop seeing a system someone shared with you, choose Leave on it. See the privacy policy for details.
