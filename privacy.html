Rotation School Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

In short

  • Rotation School has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The developer runs no servers and cannot see your data.
  • Your custom systems stay on your device unless you turn on iCloud sync. Then they are stored in your own iCloud account.
  • Rotation School is free and has no in-app purchases.

Data on your device

The custom systems you create (system and scenario names, player roles, optional player names and court positions) and your app preferences are stored on your device. They are not sent anywhere unless you turn on iCloud sync or share a system.

iCloud sync (optional, off by default)

If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your custom systems are stored in your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer cannot read this data. Apple stores it under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

If you invite people to a system, they can see that system and the name on your iCloud account. When you open a system someone shared with you, you see the owner's name, and if an editor saves a change first, you may see that editor's name. Invitations are sent through Apple's sharing interface; Rotation School does not read your contacts. Player names you type are shared with everyone you invite, so only add names you are happy to share.

Deleting your data

  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on that device.
  • In the app, Settings → Delete my cloud data removes your systems from iCloud and ends any shares you started. Systems stay on your devices.
  • To stop seeing a system someone shared with you, choose Leave on that system.
  • You can also manage iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Children

Rotation School does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
